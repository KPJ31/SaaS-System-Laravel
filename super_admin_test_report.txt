Bugs found
- Company status changes allowed repeated or invalid transitions.
- Company suspension/rejection from the company details page did not require a reason.
- Suspending a company did not suspend company users.
- Company approval/rejection emails were sent inside the database transaction, so a mail failure could roll back successful database changes.
- Payment verification allowed repeated or invalid transitions.
- Verified subscription payments did not update the related subscription.
- Subscription date validation allowed renewal/end/trial dates before the start date.
- Settings validation allowed invalid timezone, date format, and color values.
- CSV exports did not guard exported cells against spreadsheet formula injection.

Bugs fixed
- Added company status transition validation.
- Added required reason validation for company suspend/reject actions.
- Suspended active/pending company users when a company is suspended.
- Reactivated suspended company-admin users when a company is reactivated.
- Moved company approval/rejection mail sending outside the transaction and logged mail failures safely.
- Added payment status transition validation.
- Updated subscriptions when platform subscription payments are verified.
- Added subscription date ordering validation.
- Tightened system settings validation for timezone, date format, and primary color.
- Sanitized report CSV titles, filter rows, headers, and data rows.
